Welcome to my page 👋! I’m a PhD student in the UCSF–UC Berkeley Computational Precision Health ([CPH](https://computationalhealth.berkeley.edu/)) program, currently working with Dr. [Rima Arnaout](https://profiles.ucsf.edu/rima.arnaout) on deep learning methods for cardiac image analysis. My research sits at the intersection of machine learning, computer vision, and medical imaging, with a focus on building robust and clinically meaningful AI systems.

Previously, I worked at the University of Colorado Anschutz Medical Campus under Prof. [Jayashree Kalpathy-Cramer](https://som.cuanschutz.edu/Profiles/Faculty/Profile/36939), where I contributed to ophthalmic-image-focused  data science projects, including image registration, segmentation, and longitudinal disease analysis, working closely with clinicians and translational research teams. 

I also worked at Moorfields Eye Hospital under Dr. [Nikolas Pontikos](https://pontikoslab.com/team/nikolas_pontikos), on projects SynthEye and Eye2Gene, which focus on next-generation phenotyping inherited retinal diseases using deep learning and generative AI.

Outside of research, I’m a professionally-graded Indian Classical vocalist (B-high grade, All-India-Radio) and have performed in the US, UK, UAE, Malaysia, Singapore, and India.
